IntelliGuard V2 — CyberRover 'IITM Janakpuri, West Delhi, India 🇮🇳'

IntelliGuard V2 (CyberRover) '🇳 is an autonomous robotic system developed to reduce human life risks in hazardous and confined environments such as borewells, mines, and narrow spaces. The rover is designed to perform operations in place of human workers where direct access can be dangerous, helping improve safety and reduce the risk of fatalities. The project was presented at an international conference and showcased at our college.
